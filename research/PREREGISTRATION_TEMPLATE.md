# Preregistration template (fill in before a campaign)

- Campaign identifier, date, owner:
- Primary question and smallest useful effect:
- Primary comparison and primary metric:
- Corpus, origin, families, split, hashes and exclusion criteria:
- Closest existing work (version) and the difference tested:
- Resolved models, provider, parameters, harness:
- Information visible to each role, and evidence of isolation:
- Number of cases and repetitions, justification, seed, executed order:
- Per-job and total caps, judging costs, prices, authorized budget:
- Incident and retry policy, maximum number of attempts:
- Judging rubric, calibration, resolution of disagreements:
- Secondary measures, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty method:
- Stopping rules, rule for a changed model alias:
- Publication and data-sharing plan, access owner:
- Hash of the frozen protocol, optional external timestamp:
